Calibration

      
On any result under Evaluate, record the hiring manager's decision and why. Decisions that differ from Copilot are the most valuable: they are added to the prompt as worked examples, with your note as the reason, so the next evaluations lean the way the client does.

      

      
    

    

      
Add a rule

      
A general lesson in your own words, e.g. “Reject pure simulation/CFD profiles; the client wants hands-on prototyping.” It is added to this project's Calibration notes.

      

    

    

      
Distill lessons from corrections

      
Claude reads the profiles you decided on (disagreements first) with your notes and drafts short general rules. You review and edit them before they are added to Calibration notes.

Icebreakers

      
Three personalised openers for every profile the hiring manager approved.
